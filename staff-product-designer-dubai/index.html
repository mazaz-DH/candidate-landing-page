About the role

What you'll be doing

As Staff Product Designer, you'll lead design for newly formed Global Qcommerce Hub squads — owning end-to-end design processes and setting the bar for how our products look, feel, and function across dozens of markets.

Key responsibilities

  • ✓Align design strategy with product vision through cross-functional collaboration
  • ✓Transform complex user and business requirements into user-centric solutions
  • ✓Own end-to-end design: discovery, research, high-fidelity design, implementation support
  • ✓Produce production-ready web and mobile experiences in Figma
  • ✓Advocate for user needs in product discussions
  • ✓Champion design best practices and mentor team members

Where you'll sit

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Answers based on the job description — reach out to your recruiter for anything more specific.

The process has four stages:

  1. Recruiter interview (30 mins) — role fitment, motivation, communication, and a salary discussion.
  2. Portfolio / case study interview (60 mins, panel) — design expertise, partner collaboration, delivery and implementation.
  3. Whiteboarding challenge (60 mins, panel) — product thinking skills, partner collaboration, delivery and implementation.
  4. Final interview (30 mins) — vision, execution, and design leadership.

This is an on-site role based at talabat's Dubai Tech Hub. You'll be working in person with the local team while collaborating remotely with Berlin-based colleagues.

In the first 90 days, you'll ramp up on the Qcommerce Hub's products and processes, build relationships with your Berlin counterparts, and start owning end-to-end design for at least one squad. By month six, you'll be setting design direction independently and mentoring other designers on the team.

You'll partner closely with product managers, engineers, and researchers across the Qcommerce Hub squads, plus your design manager and design peers shown in the "Meet the team" section above. You'll also regularly engage with Berlin-based global teams to align on standards.

Bring a portfolio that shows end-to-end ownership — discovery through shipped product — ideally on complex, data-heavy B2B or marketplace experiences. Be ready to talk about a time you led design strategy across teams or mentored other designers, and show comfort operating in ambiguity across a large, multi-market organization.
