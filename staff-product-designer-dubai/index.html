About the role

What you'll be doing

As Staff Product Designer, you'll lead design for newly formed Global Qcommerce Hub squads — owning end-to-end design processes and setting the bar for how our products look, feel, and function across dozens of markets.

Key responsibilities

  • ✓Align design strategy with product vision through cross-functional collaboration
  • ✓Transform complex user and business requirements into user-centric solutions
  • ✓Own end-to-end design: discovery, research, high-fidelity design, implementation support
  • ✓Produce production-ready web and mobile experiences in Figma
  • ✓Advocate for user needs in product discussions
  • ✓Champion design best practices and mentor team members

Where you'll sit

  • ✓Based at talabat's Dubai Tech Hub, on-site
  • ✓Working closely with Berlin-based global teams
  • ✓Partnering with PMs, engineers, and researchers daily
  • ✓Designing for heavy, data-dense B2B and B2C surfaces
  • ✓Influencing design standards used across the region
